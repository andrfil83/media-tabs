Media Tabs helps you control audio and video playing in your browser tabs without switching between tabs.

FEATURES

• Popup list of all tabs with media players (video and audio)
• Open as a separate floating window and keep it on screen
• Play / Pause each tab individually
• Mute / Unmute each tab, with a volume slider
• Seek −5 / +5 seconds, plus a progress bar to jump to a position
• Pause all and Mute all bulk actions
• Volume control for all tabs at once
• Tabs stay in browser order (left to right)
• Highlight of the last tab you controlled
• Double-click a row to jump to that tab
• Right-click the extension icon - play/pause the last highlighted media tab

Pause or mute all tabs from one place, in a popup or a separate window.
